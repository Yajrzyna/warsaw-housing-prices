What moves prices

Interest rates & outlook

A mortgage is a long, large loan, so its cost moves a lot with the rate: at about 6% over 25 years, one point more cuts what a given payment can borrow by about 9%. Warsaw prices follow that logic only partly. The real rate (the rate minus inflation) matters much more than the nominal one, and sales volumes react faster than prices.

Median error: half of the valuations are closer than this. The data has no information about condition or interior finish, which limits accuracy.

Data & method

Data

  • Rejestr Cen Nieruchomości (RCN), Warsaw: every sale from notarial deeds, open data since 2025.
  • BDOT10k and PRG (GUGiK): buildings, storeys, transit stops, parks, district boundaries.
  • NBP BaRN housing prices: benchmark. Eurostat HICP: inflation.
  • Metro opening dates by station, so distance to metro reflects the network on each sale date.

Cleaning: apartment sales kept

Caveats

  • Deeds are registered with a lag: 2026Q2 is almost empty and excluded; series end at 2026Q1.
  • Developer prices are recorded at the final deed, usually 1–3 years after the price was agreed.
  • Building age is known only for buildings first sold new from 2007 on.
  • NBP's hedonic index grows less than every RCN-based index after 2016 (+% vs +% for 2015–2025).
  • Prices are nominal and include VAT on developer sales unless marked real.
